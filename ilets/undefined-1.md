# 문장패턴

## 📌 1) 가장 많이 등장한 ‘의견·주장’ 패턴 (빈출 최상위)

주장 제시 패턴

* Critics argue that…
* Experts warn that…
* Researchers suggest that…
* Analysts contend that…
* It has been argued that…
* Many scholars maintain that…
* Evidence suggests that…

가능성·추측 패턴

* may + 동사원형
* could potentially + 동사원형
* is likely to + 동사원형
* is unlikely to + 동사원형
* has the potential to + 동사원형

문제 제기 패턴

* raises concerns about…
* has intensified debates regarding…
* has sparked controversy / criticism…
* has complicated efforts to…

## 📌 2) 고난도 리딩에서 반복된 “원인 → 결과” 패턴

원인 표현

* due to, because of, as a result of
* driven by…, triggered by…
* stemming from…, resulting from…

결과 표현

* leading to…
* resulting in…
* giving rise to…
* contributing to…
* causing…
* undermining…

원인→결과 복합 패턴

* X has led many experts to argue that Y
* X continues to shape Y in ways that…
* X may ultimately determine whether Y succeeds

## 📌 3) 고급 논리 연결 패턴 (IELTS 7.0\~7.5에서 거의 항상 나오는 구조)

대조(Contrasting)

* However, …
* Yet, …
* Nevertheless, …
* Although / Even though / While
* In contrast, …
* Whereas…

양면성(Mixed/dual evaluation)

* While X offers benefits, it also creates significant challenges.
* Despite its potential, X remains limited by…
* Although widely praised, X may introduce risks that…

조건·가정 패턴

* Without X, Y will remain limited.
* Unless X occurs, Y is unlikely to improve.
* If X continues unchecked, Y may worsen.

## 📌 5) 리딩 난도 상승의 핵심 ‘문장 구조 패턴’

#### 복합·중첩 구조

* X has become increasingly common, as Y continues to…
* Although X has improved, Y remains problematic due to…
* While X aims to achieve Y, critics argue that…
* Because X depends heavily on Y, the effectiveness of Z is limited.

* These developments highlight the tension between…
* Such patterns reveal deeper issues surrounding…
* This raises fundamental questions about…
